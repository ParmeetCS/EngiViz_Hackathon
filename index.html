SYNTHESIS & CONCLUSIONS

Key Research Findings

FINDING 01 PRICE DYNAMICS

Tribeca Commands NYC's Highest Median Rate ($295/Night)

Across 221 verified micro-regions, Tribeca (Manhattan) leads with a median price of $295/night across 177 listings, followed by NoHo ($250) and Flatiron ($225). Luxury outlier listings up to $10,000 elevate Tribeca's average to $490.64 (+66.3% above its median).

177 Verified Units Mean: $491 vs Median: $295
FINDING 02 INVENTORY COMPOSITION

Entire Homes Dominate Market Supply (52.0%)

Entire apartments constitute NYC's predominant lodging tier with 25,409 verified listings (52.0% market share, $160 median), followed by Private rooms (22,326 listings, 45.7%, $70 median). Shared rooms represent a negligible 2.4% niche (1,160 units).

25,409 Entire Homes 52.0% Market Share · $160 Med
FINDING 03 GEOSPATIAL CONCENTRATION

North Brooklyn Captures 43.3% of Highly Reviewed Units

Out of 7,081 listings with ≥50 reviews, Brooklyn leads all boroughs with 3,065 properties (43.3%), surpassing Manhattan (2,751 units). Bedford-Stuyvesant (708) and Williamsburg (495) form the primary traveler satisfaction epicenter along the L and G subway lines.

7,081 High-Review Listings Bed-Stuy: 708 · W'burg: 495
FINDING 04 AVAILABILITY & REGULATION

Weak Positive Correlation (r = +0.14) Driven by Local Law 18

The Pearson correlation between minimum nights and annual availability is r = +0.144 (weak positive). Extended-stay listings (≥30 nights) average 224.1 available days/year vs 101.5 days for short-stay units (<30 nights), reflecting commercial host compliance rather than causation.

Pearson r = +0.144 224d vs 102d Avg Availability
FINDING 05 CALENDAR LIQUIDITY

Shared Rooms Maintain Highest Annual Availability (162.0 Days)

Shared room inventory exhibits the highest annual calendar availability at 162.0 days/year (median: 90 days), substantially exceeding Entire homes (111.9 days, median: 42) and Private rooms (111.2 days, median: 45). High persistent vacancy in shared communal spaces contrasts with the rapid booking turnover of private units.
